# T008 — MFE/MAE Tracking

Priority: P1
Status: PLANNED

## Goal
Track prospective outcomes conservatively and prevent premature success or failure claims.

## Deliverables
- evaluation horizon rules
- MFE/MAE accounting model
- outcome status policy
- OPEN / NOT_YET_MATURE handling

## Acceptance Criteria
- no observation is counted as success or failure before it is complete
- open and not-yet-mature outcomes remain explicitly labeled
- evaluation horizons and ambiguous exit rules are documented

## Validation Steps
- test unresolved observation states
- test stop/target ambiguity handling
- verify look-ahead prevention in outcome logic

## Notes
These metrics are prospective and must never be described as validated predictive performance.
